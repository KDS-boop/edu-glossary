# Keyword & Topic Map — EduGlossary

## Cluster Architecture

EduGlossary targets **informational / educational queries** in 5 technology domains. Each cluster is anchored by a category hub page and supported by glossary entries + articles.

### Cluster Priority (by search volume potential + competition feasibility)

1. **Blockchain & Decentralization** — high interest, moderate competition, strong internal linking
2. **AI & Data** — highest interest, high competition, but long-tail opportunities abundant
3. **Cybersecurity** — steady demand, moderate competition, strong featured snippet potential
4. **Cloud Computing** — enterprise-heavy, high competition, long-tail focus needed
5. **Software Development** — most competitive, but high volume; long-tail + comparison queries are key
